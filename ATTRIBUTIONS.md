# Attributions

## stb_image

The decoders and encoders this package is a binding to come from
[stb](https://github.com/nothings/stb), by Sean Barrett and contributors, which is
dual licensed as public domain or MIT. `stb_image.h` and `stb_image_write.h` are
fetched at a pinned commit by `build.lua` rather than carried in the repository, and
compiled into `stb.so` at install time.
